### Background

You have very few memories of your parents. As a young child, your family was attacked by a band of ruthless raiders while traveling through the frontier. You survived, but your parents did not. The attackers seemed strangely uninterested in wealth, spending more time searching wagons and belongings than collecting valuables before leaving everything in flames.

With nowhere else to go, you were taken in by a small temple devoted to healing and protecting those in need.

There you met another orphan named **Rowan**.

The two of you grew up together, trained together, and became close friends. While Rowan trained to become a paladin, you studied as an acolyte and healer. Together, you traveled throughout the countryside tending to the wounded after monster attacks, bandit raids, and other tragedies.

Over time, both of you came to the same troubling conclusion.

You were always arriving **too late**.

You could heal the wounded, comfort the grieving, and bury the dead—but none of it stopped the next attack.

You became convinced that protecting innocent people meant confronting evil before it reached them.

Eventually, your paths diverged.

Rowan continued his training toward becoming a paladin, while you left the temple to become a ranger, believing you could do more good protecting travelers, hunting dangerous creatures, and tracking those who preyed upon the innocent than remaining within temple walls.

Although your callings changed, your friendship with Rowan never did.

During your years traveling the frontier, you became acquainted with many priests, healers, and wandering protectors, including **Sister Garaele**, whose compassion and dedication earned your respect.

Some time later, Rowan sought you out again.

His mentor, **Sir Garrick Dawnshield**, had sent him to meet **Sildar Hallwinter** of the Lords’ Alliance to investigate the truth behind the destruction of Rowan’s tribe. Sildar agreed to help, but first asked Rowan to accompany him on one final mission for an old friend, a dwarven miner named **Gundren Rockseeker**.

Sildar also agreed to quietly investigate your own family’s murder while researching Rowan’s past. The similarities between the two tragedies troubled him enough that he believed they might somehow be connected.

Knowing Rowan would need someone he trusted, you agreed to accompany him.

Together, the two of you recovered a strange ancient puzzle box from a forgotten dwarven vault using information and tools provided by Gundren. Whatever the box is, Gundren believes it may be connected to something much larger.

Now, at Gundren’s request, the two of you are traveling to Neverwinter to meet someone Gundren believes can help unlock its secrets.
