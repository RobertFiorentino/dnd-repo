### Background

You were born into a Goliath tribe that lived in the mountains near Phandalin. Your people were skilled quarrymen, hunters, and stonemasons who lived simple but prosperous lives. Your childhood was a happy one, surrounded by family, friends, and the guidance of your wise clan elder, **Tharak**.

When you were still young, everything changed.

One day, Elder Tharak suddenly ordered the entire tribe to abandon their home. No explanation was given. Homes, quarries, and possessions were left behind as the tribe became nomadic, eventually settling near Longsaddle. You never understood why the elder made such a drastic decision and grew up believing fear had clouded his judgment.

One memory has always stayed with you. During **Greengrass of 1480**, the tribe celebrated Elder Tharak’s eightieth birthday. Everyone joked that celebrating such a milestone during Greengrass was a sign of great fortune for the years ahead.

They were wrong.

Only a few weeks later, a local noble, **Lord Alaric Hawthorne**, accused your people of poaching on his lands. His soldiers hunted your tribe down and slaughtered nearly everyone you had ever known. You survived only by fleeing into the wilderness. In your dreams, you still see Hawthorne’s round face, white bushy mustache, and hear his order to leave no one alive.

An aging paladin named **Sir Garrick Dawnshield** found you and brought you to a small temple, where he raised you alongside another orphan named **Dru Eghoe**. The two of you grew up together, studied the faith together, and became lifelong friends. While Dru trained as an acolyte and healer, you devoted yourself to the path of the paladin. Together, you traveled the countryside tending to villages after monster attacks, bandit raids, and other tragedies.

Over the years, the two of you began to share the same frustration. You were always arriving too late. You could comfort the survivors and bury the dead, but rarely stop the evil that caused the suffering in the first place. Dru eventually left the temple to become a ranger, believing he could better protect the innocent by hunting danger before it reached them. Although your paths diverged, your friendship never did.

As your own training came to an end, Sir Garrick admitted that your skills were worthy of becoming a paladin—but your heart was not yet at peace.

He told you that before swearing your sacred oath, you first had to confront your past and discover the truth of what happened to your people.

You eventually learned that **Lord Alaric Hawthorne died not long after the massacre**, robbing you of any chance for vengeance. Over time, your desire for revenge gave way to something else: understanding. Why did Elder Tharak flee the mountains near Phandalin? Why were your people accused? Why were they hunted so mercilessly? Those questions have haunted you ever since.

To aid your search, Sir Garrick introduced you to **Sildar Hallwinter** of the Lords’ Alliance. Sildar agreed to investigate not only Lord Hawthorne and the massacre, but also everything that had been happening in and around **Phandalin** during that time. If there was a larger truth behind your tribe’s flight and eventual destruction, he believed the records—and the people—might still hold the answers.

Before the two of you could begin that investigation, Sildar asked you to accompany him on one final task. He owed an old friend, **Gundren Rockseeker**, a debt, and Gundren needed help recovering a mysterious artifact. Knowing you would need someone you trusted at your side, you sought out Dru once again. Together, the three of you recovered the ancient puzzle box. Now, with that task complete, you and Sildar intend to travel to Phandalin and finally begin searching for the truth behind the tragedy that has haunted you since childhood.
