### Background

You were born and raised in **Phandalin**, where your uncle **Bart** has long been one of the town’s most respected figures. Growing up around the inn, you met all sorts of travelers, merchants, miners, and craftsmen. While most children dreamed of becoming adventurers, you were always fascinated by how things worked. Broken locks, wagon wheels, crossbows, clocks—you couldn’t resist taking them apart just to see if you could improve them before putting them back together.

Recognizing your talent, your uncle arranged for you to apprentice under the renowned gnome inventor **Professor Tinkwhistle Coppergear** in Neverwinter. Coppergear’s workshop was famous for producing everything from ingenious mining equipment to magical devices commissioned by nobles and guilds across the Sword Coast.

During your apprenticeship, you met another student named **Brog HandsomeHammer**. The two of you spent years learning together. While Brog excelled at the theory and magical side of invention, you had an instinctive knack for practical engineering and finding unconventional solutions to problems. You became good friends, even though you often approached the same challenge in very different ways.

As your apprenticeship neared its end, you realized something: you didn’t want to spend your life working in someone else’s workshop. You wanted to build something of your own.

When Brog's uncle Gundren Rockseeker heard about your plans, he immediately offered to help.

“I’ve made a fair bit of coin over the years,” he told you. “Someone once took a chance on me. Seems only fair I do the same for someone else.”

Gundren paid for a storefront in Phandalin, bought your first tools and equipment, and even covered the cost of renovating the building into a proper workshop. When you asked how you could ever repay him, he simply laughed and said you can repay him when your business is booming.

Over the past several weeks you’ve been working hard to prepare for your grand opening - "Altimawares". The sign is painted. The benches are built. Most of your equipment has arrived. You can almost picture customers walking through the front door.

Unfortunately, timing has not been on your side.

Since the disappearance of the Rockseeker brothers, the Redbrands have become increasingly bold. Merchants are afraid to travel to Phandalin. Deliveries are delayed or canceled. Local craftsmen have stopped taking work. The Redbrands have begun shaking down businesses for “protection” money—even ones that haven’t opened yet.

You’ve managed to avoid the worst of it so far largely because everyone knows you’re one of Gundren’s people. Still, every day your workshop remains closed costs you money, and you worry it’s only a matter of time before the Redbrands decide your connection to Gundren no longer protects you.

Your uncle Bart has tried to reassure you that Gundren always keeps his word and that everything will work out.

Still…

The Rockseeker brothers have been gone much longer than anyone expected.

And you’re beginning to worry that something has gone terribly wrong.
