# Thirsting Blade
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 157*  

**Prerequisite**: 5th Warlock

You gain the Extra Attack feature for your pact weapon only. With that feature, you can attack twice with the weapon instead of once when you take the [[3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions#Attack\|Attack]] action on your turn.
