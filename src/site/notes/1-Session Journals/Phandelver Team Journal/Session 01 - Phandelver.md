# **Session 1: The Road to Phandalin**

![PhandelverParty.png\|center\|800](/img/user/z_Assets/PhandelverParty.png)
Our adventure began in **Neverwinter**, where several paths unexpectedly converged.

Gundren Rockseeker was preparing to return to Phandalin with his nephew **Brog**, fresh out of school and looking for something more interesting to do with his life—provided his uncle could stop calling him **“Brogadocious.”**

Meanwhile, **Sildar Hallwinter** arrived with his former pupils **Rowan** and **Dru**, carrying the mysterious puzzle box they had recovered on Gundren’s behalf. Gundren remained frustratingly secretive about exactly why the artifact mattered, promising only that he and his brothers had discovered something important near Phandalin and that everything would be explained once they arrived safely in town.

Before that explanation could go any further, **Nissan** arrived unexpectedly from Phandalin with troubling news. The Redbrands had grown increasingly bold, the town sheriff had disappeared, and the people Nissan would normally have turned to for help—including Gundren’s brothers, who were apparently off prospecting—were nowhere to be found.

Plans quickly changed. Gundren and Sildar would ride ahead while the others followed with Gundren’s wagon and supplies.

![GundrenSildar.png\|center\|400](/img/user/z_Assets/GundrenSildar.png)

## **The Puzzle Box**

The journey began peacefully enough, but the mysterious box quickly became a source of disagreement.

Rowan had possession of it and, despite receiving no particular instruction to guard it from his companions, apparently decided that possession was nine-tenths of paladin law.

Naturally, **Brog and Dru decided to steal it from him.**

Their conspiracy was partially successful. Dru managed to get his hands on the box and began working on its strange physical mechanism, but whatever he discovered was only part of the solution. His attempt to quietly return the box to Rowan before anyone noticed was considerably less successful.

Before the dispute could be settled, however, the travelers found something far more concerning in the road ahead:

**Two dead horses.**

They belonged to Gundren and Sildar.

## **Goblins!**

The party barely had time to investigate before goblins attacked from the surrounding woods.

The fight was vicious, and when one surviving goblin fled into the forest, the party gave chase.

What followed was a frantic pursuit through the wilderness as the goblin scrambled over obstacles and through difficult terrain in an attempt to escape. The adventurers eventually caught their quarry— **Needer**.

Needer proved considerably more useful alive than dead.

Despite repeated discussions among his captors about whether they should kill him anyway, Needer eventually provided information about the Cragmaw goblins, their hideout, and the dangers along the trail. With Brog providing gold piece after gold piece, the party avoided several hazards and made their way into **Cragmaw Hideout**.

## **Sildar**

Deep inside the caves, the party finally found Sildar.

He was alive—but barely.

![Yeemik.png\|left](/img/user/z_Assets/Yeemik.png)He was being held by **Yeemik**, a goblin with ambitions of replacing the hideout’s bugbear leader, **Klarg**. Yeemik offered the adventurers a deal:

**Kill Klarg, and Sildar lives.**

The party initially chose violence instead.

When Yeemik threatened to kill Sildar unless they stopped fighting, Rowan continued his attack.

Yeemik wasn’t bluffing.

Sildar fell, grievously wounded and barely clinging to life. Yeemik put a crossbow to his throat and gave the party one final opportunity:

**Bring him Klarg’s head—or Sildar dies.**

This time, the party listened.

## **Klarg**

![bugbear-stalker.webp\|right\|300](/img/user/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fey/img/bugbear-stalker.webp)The adventurers pushed deeper into the hideout and confronted **Klarg**, the bugbear commanding the Cragmaw goblins.

During the confrontation, they heard troubling new names: **King Grol** and someone or some thing known only as **the Black Spider**. Whatever had happened to Gundren, it was becoming increasingly clear that his capture might not have been a simple roadside robbery.

Klarg didn’t live long enough to explain much more.

The party defeated him, claimed his head, and returned to fulfill Yeemik’s bargain.

Sildar survived—but Gundren was nowhere to be found.

And somewhere during the chaos, Needer took one look at Klarg’s ferocious wolf and decided that his promising new career as an adventurer’s prisoner had reached its natural conclusion.

He ran.

## **Where Things Stand**

The party has survived its first encounter with the Cragmaw goblins and rescued Sildar, though he remains badly injured.

Gundren is still missing.

The mysterious puzzle box remains unopened.

The names **King Grol** and **the Black Spider** now hang over Gundren’s disappearance.

And Phandalin still waits ahead—with a missing sheriff, increasingly dangerous Redbrands, and problems that have only grown worse while everyone has been away.

For now, one thing is clear:

Whatever Gundren discovered, someone else wants it badly enough to take him for it.
