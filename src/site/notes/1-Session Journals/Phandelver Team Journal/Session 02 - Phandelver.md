# **Session 2: Welcome to Phandalin**

The party finally arrived in Phandalin, planning to head straight to the Stonehill Inn to see Nissan’s uncle Bart. They didn’t make it far.

## Something Is Wrong
![Sister Garaele.png\|left\|200](/img/user/z_Assets/Sister%20Garaele.png)Still badly injured from Cragmaw Hideout, Sildar collapsed from the cart. Brog initially tried to help, but Dru quickly realized his old mentor needed more than field medicine. He directed the group to **Sister Garaele**, Phandalin’s healer and an old acquaintance of his.

The party was immediately impressed by Garaele’s beauty, although she was far too concerned with Sildar to notice. As she attempted to heal him, however, something went terribly wrong. Garaele suddenly let out an unnatural, almost supernatural shriek and found herself unable to continue. She assured the group she could stabilize Sildar, but asked them to fetch Molly from the **Stonehill Inn** while she rested.

At the inn, the party finally met Nissan’s uncle **Bart**, who was sporting a black eye and dried blood from his own recent encounters with the **Redbrands**. Bart introduced his young halfling assistant manager, **Molly**, paid the party for delivering Gundren’s supplies, and also rewarded them for recovering the stolen provisions from Cragmaw Hideout.

Nissan proudly produced Klarg’s severed head to see whether his uncle recognized the bugbear. Bart did not.

While the others unloaded supplies, Nissan noticed something considerably more interesting: **a dark figure** watching them from a nearby rooftop. The figure disappeared almost immediately. The party searched the area and even entered the house beneath where the stranger had been standing, only to find an elderly woman attempting to sell them sewing wares.

![BartMolly.png\|center\|700](/img/user/z_Assets/BartMolly.png)
## Old Mysteries, New Leads
When they returned to Stonehill, Molly had come back with good news: Sildar was going to recover. She also proved to be an unexpectedly valuable source of local information. A **creepy stranger** had recently been digging around the graveyard and stealing bones. Sister Garaele’s **special abilities** had stopped working properly. And Molly herself had previously spoken with a mysterious woman dressed in black who liked to travel across the rooftops.

![Rusk.png\|right\|200](/img/user/z_Assets/Rusk.png)Before they could make much sense of any of this, a filthy, sweaty Redbrand named **Rusk** entered and demanded free drinks. When Rusk began putting his hands on Molly, the party intervened. A fight nearly broke out, although it mostly amounted to shoving, threats, and spilled beer before Bart stepped in and intimidated Rusk into leaving.

Rusk left humiliated, but not before taking a long look at the newcomers who had embarrassed him.

The encounter convinced Bart that things in Phandalin were getting worse. Gundren was missing, the other Rockseeker brothers—Tharden and Nundro—had been gone too long, and the Redbrands were becoming increasingly brazen. Bart decided to head out himself to find the brothers and bring them home, asking Nissan to keep an eye on Molly while he was gone.

The party briefly considered seeking out the Redbrands themselves, but first returned to check on Sildar.

![Sildar.png\|left](/img/user/z_Assets/Sildar.png)Sildar was awake and already thinking about what had happened at Cragmaw Hideout. Unfortunately, the party’s victory had created a new problem: Klarg was dead, and with him potentially their best source of information about where Gundren had been taken. Sildar reminded Rowan and Dru of lessons he had taught them years earlier. A sword might solve an immediate problem, but diplomacy, restraint, and careful thinking could sometimes accomplish far more. The next person endangered by acting too quickly might not be a goblin or bugbear. It might be an innocent—or even a child.

That was when Dru remembered the severed head.

Sister Garaele possessed the ability to speak with the dead. If she could question Klarg, perhaps death hadn’t cost them their lead after all.

Garaele warned them that something had been interfering with her powers, but agreed to try for Gundren’s sake. The attempt went disastrously wrong. Whatever supernatural force had been troubling her suddenly overwhelmed her. Garaele collapsed, her eyes turned white, and for a moment something else seemed to look out through them.

It spoke a single word:

**“Mine.”**

When Garaele recovered, she finally told them what had been happening. For months she had dreamed of an ancient witch named Agatha, whom she believed haunted the swamps near Conyberry. The attacks were getting worse, and now whatever Agatha was doing seemed capable of interfering with Garaele’s magic directly.

Suddenly dealing with Agatha became much more than helping a local priestess. If they could free Garaele from whatever was tormenting her, she might regain the power to question Klarg—and Klarg might tell them where to find Gundren.

There was another mystery waiting for them as well.

## The Puzzle Box
Sildar asked whether Brog and Nissan had made any progress with Gundren’s puzzle box. Working together, the two tinkerers finally deciphered its mechanisms and magical runes. They aligned the symbols to spell **PHANDELVER** in Dwarvish.

The box opened.

Unfortunately, inside was another puzzle.

This mechanism required some sort of **word code**.

Sildar recognized the significance of the word Phandelver and explained what he knew. Long ago, humans, gnomes, and dwarves had worked together under the Phandelver Pact at Wave Echo Cave, where enormous mineral wealth was accompanied by something even more extraordinary: the legendary Forge of Spells, capable of creating powerful magical items. Eventually, orc raiders and mercenary wizards attacked in an attempt to seize that wealth, destroying the prosperous settlement of old Phandalin in the process.

![Harbin.png\|right](/img/user/z_Assets/Harbin.png)Sildar suspected his missing friend Iarno might know considerably more.

The party accompanied him to see Townmaster Harbin Wester, where Sildar could begin searching local records for information about Iarno as well as the old events surrounding the tragedies that had destroyed Rowan’s tribe and Dru’s family.

Harbin proved less interested in confronting Phandalin’s immediate problems. He downplayed the threat posed by the Redbrands and instead tried to direct the adventurers toward a dangerous group of orc bandits operating out of Wyvern Tor.

Eventually, the party decided they had heard enough. It was time to confront the Redbrands.

## This Town Ain't Big Enough
Unfortunately, the Redbrands had reached the same conclusion about them.

Rusk returned with three companions, led by the calm and charismatic Verren. Rather than immediately draw weapons, Verren politely explained that Rusk believed the newcomers had disrespected him and suggested that a simple apology could make the entire misunderstanding disappear.

![Verren 1.png\|left\|200](/img/user/z_Assets/Verren%201.png)Surprisingly, the apology came fairly easily.

But nobody relaxed.

The two groups stood facing one another in the street as the tension continued to build. Someone suggested drinks. The Redbrands liked that idea, and Molly was sent inside to fetch beer for everyone.

For a moment, it almost seemed possible that nobody would draw a weapon.

Then Molly came hurrying outside with a tray of drinks and accidentally spilled the entire thing over Rusk.

Everyone laughed.

Rowan looked at the soaked Redbrand and joked:

“Drinks are on you, Rusk.”

Then Rusk backhanded Molly.

The little halfling went flying, landing bloody in the street.

The laughter stopped.

Every sword came out.

The Redbrands landed some painful blows early, but the party quickly turned the fight around. Soon the surviving gang members were running for their lives. Verren nearly escaped—until somewhere out of sight the party heard a magical zap followed by a scream.

## The Woman in Black
![Hooded Figure.png\|right\|200](/img/user/z_Assets/Hooded%20Figure.png)When they investigated, they found the **mysterious hooded woman** they had seen on the rooftops.

Verren stood helpless beside her, magically frozen in place.

The woman explained that she had been watching the party and offered Verren to them as a gesture of good faith. She knew they possessed the puzzle box, and she wanted it. More precisely, **her employer** wanted it—and her employer was sufficiently well connected that almost anything could be offered in exchange.

Money.

Information.

**Perhaps even a person**.

Nissan responded to this intriguing proposition by inexplicably attempting to kiss her.

She responded with a Magic Missile to his face that nearly killed him.

Brog was considerably more cautious. He refused to make any agreement and asked how they could contact her if they decided they were interested.

The woman told them to simply let the halfling girl know.

She would hear about it.

Then she disappeared.

Her spell released, and Verren immediately began screaming for **Glasstaff**, repeatedly insisting that Glasstaff needed to know what had happened.

The party dragged their new prisoner back to Nissan’s unfinished shop.

## Where Things Stand
As the sun went down on Phandalin, the party had Verren in their custody, Gundren still missing, Sister Garaele being slowly overtaken by something called Agatha, a second mystery hidden inside the puzzle box, Sildar beginning to investigate Iarno's disappearance and two tragedies from Rowan and Dru’s past, the Rockseeker brothers missing somewhere outside town, and a mysterious woman in black offering the party virtually anything they could ask for in exchange for one very important box.

But first, Verren has some questions to answer.
