# A Choice of Two Paths

![Suldanessellar-destroyed.png\|right\|400](/img/user/z_Assets/Suldanessellar-destroyed.png)As the party licked their woulds from the battle at the Wardstones, [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Ragnar\|Ragnar]] noticed a map underneath one of the stones as well as a mysterious scroll. The group then headed back to [[2-World/Suldanessellar\|Suldanessellar]] which had been devastated by the drow attack. Ash and bitter sap hung heavy in the air.

[[3-Mechanics/NPCs/Elder Faelar Silverbranch\|Elder Faelar Silverbranch]] informed the party that while they were protecting the Wardstones, the drow pillaged the city and kidnapped many elven families. There was also indication that [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Cinder Belabranta\|Cinder]]'s brother [[3-Mechanics/NPCs/Titus Belabranta\|Titus Belabranta]]  was behind paid mercenaries who contributed to the destruction.

![HeartShard.png\|left\|200](/img/user/z_Assets/HeartShard.png)On examination of the map found at the Wardstones, they discovered that it led to the ruins of  [[2-World/Val Sharun\|Val Sharun]], a religious site once associated with the elven goddess [[2-World/Faerûn/Sehanine Moonbow\|Sehanine Moonbow]]. The Elder revealed a fragment of the Heart of Val Sharun, which wards off the pestilence caused by [[2-World/Faerûn/Talona\|Talona]]. When the heart weakens, the wards fail and with it comes the plague.  Titus aims to use the heart to control the region.

Two paths were presented to the heroes on what to pursue next:

1. Follow the drow into the Underdark where the kidnapped elves were taken, presumably for enslavement. This path has the added bonus of [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Renan\|Renan]] finally being able to redeem himself and attempt to rescue his friend [[3-Mechanics/NPCs/Kaelen Duskwhisper\|Kaelen Duskwhisper]] who was imprisoned years earlier. [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Brerik\|Brerik]] also has family held by the drow.
2. Go to [[2-World/Val Sharun\|Val Sharun]] and get the Heart before the drow or Titus get there.

After some deliberation, the party decided to take path two, since failure to secure the Heart risks all the lives along The Sword Coast. During their discussions, the group filled the newcomers in on what happened during their adventures thus far. They told [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Corin SwiftShadow\|Corin]] the fate that befell his brother [[3-Mechanics/NPCs/Laverneous\|Laverneous]], who died a hero.

![earth-elemental.webp\|right\|300](/img/user/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/img/earth-elemental.webp) As the group followed the map to a cave entrance, tunnels twisted and gave way to shattered runestone. The walls showed signs of corruption, and strange glyphs appeared on the pillars. A huge [[3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/earth-elemental-xmm\|Sentinal]] rose from the earth and barred the party from entrance. When violence seemed imminent, [[1-Party/The Plague Wardens/Ragnar\|Ragnar]] produced the scroll found at the Wardstones that let him communicate with this imposing creature.

The Sentinel spoke, saying the drow and beasts serve the corruption, and that only the pure of heart may touch the stone. Ragnar convinced the Sentinel that the group was worthy and had the best interest of the Heart. "Take this earth and dust", the Sentinel said, along with cryptic references that "glyphs were fading, drow assassins, corrupted beasts, pool of forbidden knowledge..."

The next chamber was large and had columns throughout. The party had a sense that an ambush was coming and they were right. Several drow as well as corrupted dog beasts attacked from all angles. The group made quick work  of their enemies, but it was clear the real fight had not even begun.
